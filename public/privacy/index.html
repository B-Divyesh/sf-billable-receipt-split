Privacy

Effective 28 August 2026

Your receipt data stays local

Billable Split keeps receipt images, supplier details, item lines, job splits, and history in your browser. It does not upload that information. It has no advertising, tracking, or third-party runtime scripts.

Backups

Backups are created on your device and protected with your password before download. Your password is never sent to Billable Split. We cannot recover a lost password.

Licenses

When you buy or verify a license, your browser contacts Sociobot with the license token. Sociobot and Dodo handle checkout under their own privacy terms. Your browser stores the token and a cached result.

Deletion

Use Delete permanently to remove a receipt, its image, splits, and history from this browser. Clearing site data also removes local records and the saved license. Deleted data can only be recovered from a backup you created.

Contact

Send privacy questions through sociobot.in.
