The service

Billable Split records receipt images, splits receipt lines, and exports job cost records. It does not read receipt text automatically, reconcile bank accounts, do bookkeeping, or give tax advice. Review every amount and export before relying on it.

Free and paid use

The free version stores five complete receipts. CSV and PDF exports, backups, restoration, and deletion remain available. A $19 one-time license removes the receipt limit on verified devices.
